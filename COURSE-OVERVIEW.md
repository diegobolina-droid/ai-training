### Is this course right for me?

**You should take this course if:**
- You can code proficiently in at least one language
- You want to build AI applications, not just use them
- You're committed to 5 full days of intensive learning
- You want production-ready skills, not just theory

**This course might not be for you if:**
- You've never programmed before
- You're looking for ML/AI theory (neural networks, backprop)
